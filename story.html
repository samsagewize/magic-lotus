Chapter I

The Story

Every piece starts with a blank page and a handful of color pencils. No digital tools, no undo, just pressure, patience, and layer after layer of color.

Each line, dot, and swirl is drawn by hand, building creatures, shells, and gardens that feel like they grew on the paper rather than being planned. These works live somewhere between nature and dreams: part ocean, part garden, all heart.

Every piece is a one-of-one original: a 1/1, hand-drawn in color pencil on paper. There are no editions and no reprints.
